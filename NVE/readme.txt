Data owner: Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate (NVE).

Citation in presentations:
--------------------------
Please refer to "Data: NVE" when using data or plots in presentations.
You may also add the link to the service, e.g. glacier.nve.no/viewer/CI/

Citation in publications:
-------------------------
When using the data in publications, please cite the service:
NVE, yyyy. Norwegian Water Resources and Energy Directorate (NVE). Climate indicator products, http://glacier.nve.no/viewer/CI/, 
downloaded <yyyy.mm.dd>.
(yyyy=year, mm=month, dd=day)

For mass balance and length change data: Please also cite the report series 'Glaciological investigations in Norway', 
(pdfs available at https://www.nve.no/hydrology/glaciers/glaciological-investigations-in-norway)
e.g.: Kjoellmoen et al. (2017) with updates NVEs database.

B. Kjoellmoen (Ed.), L.M. Andreassen, H. Elvehoey, M. Jackson and K. Melvold, 2017: 
Glaciological investigations in Norway in 2016. NVE Report 76 2017, 95 p. +app.

---
See also the csv-file massbalanceData_<ID>_<Glacier> for further references on individual mass balance years.
